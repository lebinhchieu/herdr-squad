## Design notes
- **AC5 over AC2/AC3 wording.** A composed file for `architecture: no`/absent must be byte-identical, so no existing block's text or `when:` changed. Every architecture change is a new block, `when: architecture = yes`, with a letter-suffixed ID next to its neighbour: SIZE-3a flag (CORE-3's owner line is not edited; core.md is at its 40-line cap, WHO-2e carries the owner delta), SIZE-9a role, SIZE-22a (rows replace the same-named SIZE-22 rows), WHO-2e (replaces the "Irreversible" rows/cells of WHO-2/WHO-2d), SPAWN-4a (also names the SPAWN-2a/b/c addition), START-4a `arch`, START-5a lifetime row, START-20 start, LC-11c (S, M) and LC-11d (L) plan gate, LC-19c Developer, SIZE-23c/23d and REV-22a conformance (M QA whitebox; L Tech Lead + QA whitebox; S review subagent), MSG-20 feedback (`ASK` the Architect, 2 rounds then Manager), MSG-20a/b Architect doubts and final. Counter: override rows instead of a `no`/`yes` twin of SIZE-22/WHO-2/WHO-2d (stated once, no duplicate sentences).
- `architect` added to the `roles:` of every unconditional rule that lists `designer` (cost, files FILE-11, LC-25, LC-26, messages, notes, safety, START-6/7/9): other roles see no change.
- **Counter, T4: `bin/squadcompose.py`** (one expression). The manager/tech-lead "Index of other rules" lists rules not chosen, with their roles; it would leak the new rule IDs and `architect` into `no` scopes. The index now skips rules whose `when` names `architecture` and hides `architect` unless the scope has `architecture: yes`. Also `tests/rulesview.py` gets a keyword-only `architecture="no"` for `composed()`.
- `architect.json`: designer.json's `scopes/*/*.md` deny would shadow `architecture.md` (a deny wins), so it is replaced by denies for `scope.md`, `roster.md`, `escalations.md`; allow is `architecture.md` and `tasks/**` only.
- `templates/squad/process.md` (v6) is only the adopt line-map source, not composed: untouched. CORE-3 unchanged (see above). README/CONTEXT/ADR/version are 03.
- Dev rules size: with the flag on, Developer rules grow 186 B at M/L (under 5 KiB) and 340 B at S; S + `design: yes` + flag is 5.4 KiB, over the 5120 B limit that `test_dev_rules_size` holds for flag-off scopes (baseline S + design 5113). Flag-off unaffected. Flagged to QAs.
