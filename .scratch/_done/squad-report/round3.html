Q14 · Who reads the report, and the headline numbers

Reader: you, at scope close or check-in, asking why was this slow or expensive?; the Manager at retro. Header KPIs: elapsed, agent-time, est. cost, returns, % attributed, PO wait, plus one new one: cost per PASSed task (cost divided by tasks that reached PASS), so two scopes of different size compare.

➡️ Yes: PO and Manager are the readers; KPI set as listed incl. cost per PASSed task.

Q15 · Two clocks: elapsed and agent-time (Designer Q-D1 merged)

Parallel agents make the two differ 3-5x. Never show a bare "time". Big number = elapsed (wall clock), under it agent-time and parallelism (agent-time / elapsed), e.g. 38h agent-time, 9 agents, ×3.2. Parallelism answers did the Developer cap get used?

➡️ Yes: elapsed big, agent-time + parallelism under it, always labelled.

Q16 · New signals the data already holds (+ Designer Q-D4, Tech Lead T1)

Found in win-compat: escalation E6 waited 84 min for you, longer than most tasks. All derivable without new hooks: PO wait (escalation open: raised → answered; elapsed intervals, never agent cost; own colour in the timeline, same "blocking" bucket in the data, a KPI line "waiting on PO Xh"); review wait (HANDIN → PASS/FAIL); iteration as a dimension (win-compat had 5); respawns and cache-expiry cost (sessions per agent − 1).

T1 catch: in win-compat 11.2 of 29.3 agent-hours (38%) were agents sitting between your typed chat messages (overnight, between rounds) with no escalation open; only 0.8 h overlapped an open escalation. Counting that as "blocked on PO" would blame you for time you were away; hiding it as plain idle hides your chat latency. Proposal: escalation open = blocking on PO; otherwise idle with reason "waiting for your chat reply", a sub-line under idle.

➡️ Yes: add all four signals to v1; escalation open = PO wait, typed-reply gaps = idle sub-line.

Q17 · Digest flags and thresholds (+ Designer Q-D5)

Digest = at most 8 findings ranked by share of its own total (each names its top item, e.g. the top 3 tasks), no absolute cut-offs, so it never goes silent or noisy. Only two thresholds: a task with 3+ returns is flagged (matches the Manager rule); a banner when under 60% attributed (agent-time) says the task table is weak. Signals: top cost tasks, role cost share, scope-overhead share, idle share, longest blocks (who waited on whom), PO wait, review wait, tasks by returns, rework cost, respawns, parallelism.

➡️ Yes: ranked digest of up to 8 findings, thresholds only at 3 returns and 60% attributed.

Q19 · Backfill old scopes

squad-report --all builds data.json for every existing per-scope scope (live and _done) whose transcripts still exist; skipped ones are listed, existing data untouched. Cheap (zero LLM) and it gives the rollup and the later "vs other scopes" a baseline: ~10 scopes already in _done.

➡️ Yes: ship --all; "vs other scopes" waits until it has run once.

Q20 · Default metric of the task × role grid and share bars (Designer Q-D2)

Cost by default, with a 3-way toggle cost / time / tokens. Your first question is money.

➡️ Yes: cost default, toggle for time and tokens.

Q21 · Overnight gaps in the per-agent timeline (Designer Q-D3)

Gaps over 1 h compress to a marker by default, with a toggle to true scale. A 3-day scope is otherwise ~70% blank.

➡️ Yes: compress gaps over 1 h, toggle to true scale.

Q22 · Rollup rows link to each scope's report (Designer Q-D6)

Relative link to the scope's report.html (scopes keep their path under scopes/_done); a disabled "no report" state when missing.

➡️ Yes: relative links plus disabled state.

Q23 · Same ticket, several names (Tech Lead T2)

In one scope a ticket is called T0, 07 and 07-squadio-helper. Without merging, one task splits into several rows and takes its returns and cost with it. Proposal: merge only when one message names both (07-squadio-helper (T0)); show leftovers separately and lower % attributed. No process.md change. (Alternative: a one-line rule "task id = ticket number" removes the cause for future scopes.)

➡️ Yes: merge when one message names both; no process.md change.

Q24 · Claude Code's own cost figure beside the estimate (Tech Lead T3)

Transcripts hold the harness's reported cost for 473 of 480 sessions; our list-price estimate differs by a median 7-10% per session. Proposal: one small line next to the headline cost, "Claude Code reported $X (estimate off by Y%)", hidden when absent. It tells you how far to trust the estimate.

➡️ Yes: show the reported figure and the delta when present.

Q25 · Reports stay local (Tech Lead T4)

report/ lives inside the scope folder, which is git-ignored, and moves to _done with it. Proposal: local and disposable (rebuild any time, never committed); --all writes report/ into _done scopes. Say if you want closed scopes' data.json committed as history.

➡️ Yes: local, disposable, never committed.
