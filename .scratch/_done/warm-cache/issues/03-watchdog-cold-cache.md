## Problem
A session idle for over an hour loses its 1h prompt cache and pays a cold write on its next turn. At 55 minutes idle, with a context over 50k and work open, the watchdog has the agent write its notes (warm, cheap), then respawns it lazily (ticket 01), so nothing is written until a message arrives (ticket 02 adds the instruction).

## AC
1. A pass `cold_cache_pass` runs in `tick()` after the idle/wake events, only when herdr answered, over the roster rows of its own home (goal home: its own roster only, not `scope_homes`). A failure in it logs `cold-cache: pass failed: <error>` and never stops the idle/wake pass. `SQUAD_WATCHDOG_COLD=0` turns it off. `decide()` and `watchdog.state.json` are not changed (existing tests green without edits to their expectations).
2. Idle and context (stories 21-23): idle = herdr state not `working` and last main-thread turn (`side == False` rows of `squadtranscript.read_session`, session id from the roster Session column) at least `COLD_IDLE_MIN` (55) minutes old; subagent turns do not count; context = that turn's input + cache read + cache write (the reader's `ctx`). No second transcript reader. Tests: last turn 54 min ago and 56 min ago; a subagent row newer than the main turn does not hide idleness.
3. Eligible (stories 1-4, 9, 16): idle, context over `COLD_CTX` (50k), work open. Prompt text and log line as in the design notes; the agent is prompted (`send`, once per message, never into a `blocked` pane: held, not counted). A Manager and a Team Lead over the floor are respawned whether or not work is open.
4. Notes check (stories 6, 24): respawn only when `mtime(roles/<name>.notes.md) > prompt_ts` and the agent is idle/done; then `squad-respawn <name> --lazy` through the injectable `respawn=` with `SQUAD_ROLE`, `SQUAD_NAME`, `SCOPE_DIR` and `GOAL_DIR` removed from the child's environment, then `SQUAD_DIR` and exactly one of `SCOPE_DIR` (scope home) or `GOAL_DIR` (goal home) set, and a timeout of `COLD_RESPAWN_TIMEOUT_S` (120; a timeout is `respawn lazy failed: timeout`); log `cold-cache: respawned lazy <name>`; entry deleted. Respawn exit not 0: log `respawn lazy failed`, no retry loop. Never `--force`. Test: the fake receives exactly those arguments and an environment without `SQUAD_ROLE`/`SQUAD_NAME`, with the parent's `SCOPE_DIR` and `SQUAD_ROLE=team-lead` set in the test's own environment; in the goal-home case `SCOPE_DIR` is absent and `GOAL_DIR` is the goal home.
5. Two prompts, then give up (stories 18, 26): first prompt, a later pass at least `COLD_RETRY_S` after it with notes still not newer prompts once more, then logs `gave up: notes not written` and does nothing more in that spell; a new last turn after `gave_up_ts` starts a new spell. Our own prompt's turn does not reset the count (design notes, C4 shape). Test: notes never written, five passes, exactly two prompts and one give-up line, no respawn.
6.
