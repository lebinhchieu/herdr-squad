squad-tabs, round 3: herdr can’t mute only the squad panes

You asked to set this up only for squad agents and leave other sessions alone. herdr 0.9.4 doesn’t allow that: its toast setting is one switch for every pane. So there are two real routes: switch herdr’s toast off and let our hook send the toasts, with your other sessions getting the same toasts as today (A), or leave herdr alone and live with squad workers pinging you (B). I recommend A. Asking herdr for a per-pane mute (C) is a request outside the squad and can go in on top of either.
